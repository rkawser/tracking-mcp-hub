# TikTok Ads

## Official — TikTok for Business MCP
- Announced at TikTok World (May 2026); now offered as a ready-made connector in Claude, Perplexity and other registries.
- **Access:** campaign management, performance reporting, creative management.
- **Info:** https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/tiktok-agentic-hub-ai-agents-skills-mcp
- Easiest path: add the TikTok Ads connector from the Claude connector directory.
